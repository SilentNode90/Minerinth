# 🌱 MineRinth

### Your Minecraft universe, all in one place.

MineRinth is a desktop launcher and content manager for Minecraft, inspired by the Modrinth ecosystem. Its goal is to bring your Minecraft content, installations, customization and entertainment together in one application.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔍 Content & Discovery
- 🔍 **Explore** — Discover Minecraft content.
- 📚 **Library** — Keep your content organized.
- 📦 **Installations** — Manage your Minecraft installations.
- 🧩 **Mods** — Browse and manage mods.
- 🎨 **Resource Packs** — Organize your resource packs.
- 🗂️ **Data Packs** — Manage datapacks.
- 🌅 **Shaders** — Organize your shaders.
- 📦 **Modpacks** — Manage modpacks.
- 🔌 **Plugins** — Manage plugins.

### 🎮 Minecraft Management
- 🌐 **Servers** — Keep track of your servers.
- 👤 **Skins** — Customize and preview Minecraft skins.
- ⭐ **Favorites** — Keep your favorite content close.
- 🕘 **History** — Access your recently viewed content.

### 🎵 Music Player
- 🎧 Integrated music player.
- 💿 Minecraft-inspired album artwork.
- ⏯️ Playback controls.
- 🔊 Volume control.
- 🔁 Repeat playback.
- 📀 Searchable music library.
- 🎼 Minecraft soundtrack collection.

### 🎨 Customization
- Themes — Customize the interface.
- Dark interface with blue and purple accents.
- Pixel-inspired typography and colorful icons.

---

## 🎨 Design

MineRinth features a distinctive visual identity combining:

- Dark navy and black backgrounds.
- Blue and purple accent colors.
- Pixel-inspired typography.
- Colorful Minecraft-style icons.
- A clean, organized desktop interface.
- Retro gaming aesthetics.

Designed to feel like a natural part of the Minecraft universe.

---

## 🛠️ Project

MineRinth is an independent project currently under development.

The application aims to provide a unified experience for discovering, organizing and managing Minecraft content.

Features, functionality and availability may change as development progresses.
